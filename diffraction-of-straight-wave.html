About this model and how to use it

Teaching reference: DSEPHY_TE_3B05, printed pp. 58–60: Experiment 5d, Table 5.3a and Figs. 5.3c–d. The diagrams here are newly drawn. Use continuous waves to compare spacing; pause and step to follow a wavefront reaching an edge.

Idealised shallow-water approximation: Depth is fixed at h = 0.01 m everywhere. With g = 9.81 m s⁻², v = √(gh) ≈ 31.3 cm/s. Wavelength is set directly; f = v/λ and the emission interval is λ/v. Changing wavelength changes spacing and emission frequency, while speed stays constant. Diffraction does not change frequency, speed or wavelength.

Wavefront construction: unobstructed straight sections join circular sections centred on barrier edges. Each curved section starts at contact and expands at v. Following the schematic Huygens construction, the extent of the drawn arcs increases smoothly with wavelength relative to the opening or obstacle. This extent is an illustration, not a calculated diffraction angle or intensity distribution. The single-edge example uses a fixed drawing scale for this comparison.

Single pulse: shows one representative crest from the selected wave train. The selected wavelength sets its illustrative spreading. The wavelength and derived frequency readouts describe that train; they are not the spectrum of an isolated physical pulse. Wavelength markers are hidden in this mode because there are no adjacent crests.

Limits: transmission strength through extremely narrow gaps is outside this simplified model; there is no imposed abrupt cutoff. Partial or full reflection from barriers, detailed interference, amplitude changes and reflections from tank walls are omitted. Where edge constructions meet behind an obstacle, they are joined into a schematic front; this is not an interference calculation.

Controls: changing a physical parameter, arrangement or wave mode restarts the sequence without changing play/pause. Geometry labels, wave direction arrows and wavelength markers have independent display toggles; none restarts the animation. Step advances by 0.05 simulation seconds and pauses. Playback speed changes timing only. Gap and obstacle widths are remembered separately while this page is open. No installation or internet connection is needed.
