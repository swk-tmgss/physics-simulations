About the model and how to use it

This is an idealised shallow-water model: v = √(gh), with g = 9.81 m/s² and depth h in metres; λ = v/f. It illustrates the textbook relationships, rather than predicting every ripple-tank condition. The whole tank has one depth, so reflection does not change the medium or wave speed.

Each line represents a crest wavefront. A single pulse shows one travelling front; frequency and wavelength apply to the continuous wave train. Teal and orange lines show the two components separately, including where they overlap. Their interference and resulting water height are not drawn. The barrier is treated as long, with no edge diffraction, transmission or losses; waves leave the visible tank without reflecting at other edges.

Changing frequency, depth, angle or wave mode restarts the sequence and keeps the current play/pause state. Restart keeps your settings. Step pauses the animation and advances simulated time by 0.05 s. Playback speed affects viewing time only. At 0°, the waves return along the same path. All controls work with a keyboard.
