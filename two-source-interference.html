Physics and how to use this version

Reference: DSEPHY_TE_3B05, printed pp. 63–71, especially the wavefront and path-difference diagrams and the nodal/antinodal guides on pp. 68–69. All diagrams here are newly drawn.

What the circles mean: each solid circle joins points at a crest of one source's contribution, +1 cm; each dashed circle joins its troughs, −1 cm. They are not resultant-wave contours. A crossing of two crests gives +2 cm, two troughs gives −2 cm, and a crest with a trough gives zero. The orange particle shows the actual resultant at P. A zero displacement at one instant alone does not prove destructive interference throughout the cycle.

Model: sources S₁ and S₂ are at (−D/2, 0) and (+D/2, 0), coherent and in phase. At any point, distances r₁ and r₂ give Δ = |r₂ − r₁|. With v = 30 cm/s, A = 1 cm and f = v/λ, yⱼ = A cos(2πft − 2πrⱼ/λ) and y = y₁ + y₂. Resultant amplitude is 2A |cos(πΔ/λ)|. Crests have radii r = vt + nλ; troughs have r = vt + (n + 0.5)λ, for integers n giving non-negative radii. These are established wave trains without a startup front.

Idealisation: equal amplitudes everywhere, no attenuation, no boundary reflections and no startup transients. Real circular waves generally weaken with distance. Particle displacement is exaggerated in the separate vertical-motion indicator.

Guides: antinodes satisfy Δ = nλ, including A₀ at Δ = 0; nodes satisfy Δ = (n + ½)λ. Both guide sets can be shown together. Both sides and all physically possible orders are available. Guides are hyperbolas for 0 < Δ < D and limiting rays for Δ = D. Snapping uses full-precision geometry, not rounded readouts.

Snapping: all five buttons can be turned on or off independently. If Solid + solid, Dashed + dashed or Solid + dashed is selected, P snaps only to the nearest selected kind of intersection, even when line buttons are also selected. Solid + solid chooses two crests (+2 cm at placement); Dashed + dashed chooses two troughs (−2 cm at placement); Solid + dashed chooses a crest and a trough from either source (zero at placement). Selected nodal and antinodal guides remain visible but do not add line targets while intersection snapping is active. If no intersection button is selected, P snaps to the selected nodal lines, antinodal lines, or the nearest of either when both are selected. With all five off, P moves freely. P stays at the same tank coordinates during playback, stepping and time shortcuts; snapping happens when selecting an option, placing P or changing physical settings. Troughs stay visible while Solid + dashed or Dashed + dashed is selected; their previous visibility returns only after both are off. Required guides stay visible while their line options are selected and return to their previous display settings when deselected. Distance visibility remains independent.

Fixed observation point: the top-down marker P keeps its position while the separate particle indicator shows vertical displacement at that location. Crest–crest and trough–trough snapping give fixed antinodes; crest–trough snapping gives a fixed node. With all three intersection options off, the line buttons constrain P to the selected lines. The time graphs describe the water at P’s selected location.

Motion: the simulation starts paused at 0.25×. Play continues until paused. Restart resets time and preserves play/pause. Step advances 0.05 s and pauses. Wave 1 crest at P pauses at its next crest, or the current instant if it is already at a crest. Advance ¼ cycle advances T/4 and pauses. Playback, stepping, restarting and both time shortcuts keep P at the same location in every mode. Slow motion affects timing only, not wave speed, wavelength or interference.

Distance labels: S₁P and S₂P are shown in centimetres and multiples of λ. The path difference is Δ = |S₂P − S₁P|. Labels move below the diagram when space is limited. Show distances and path difference hides the measurement arrows, labels and numerical distance readouts without changing the simulation.

Other controls: wavelength/separation changes restart time while preserving play/pause and P where permitted. If a source moves onto P, P moves to the nearest permitted position. Drag P or click the tank; focus it and use arrow keys to request 1 cm movements; snapping may move P further. Moving P, selecting a snap mode, changing playback speed and display toggles preserve time. Physical changes retain the selected snap options and find the nearest valid position at t = 0. Hiding troughs does not remove their physical contribution. The optional graphs show one period with a repeating phase cursor. All code is embedded; no network connection or installation is required.
